<PatchNoteSectionHeading
  icon='📤'
  title='Completely Redesigned Bulk Wine Transfer'
  label={['update']}
/>

- The original bulk wine transfer feature was difficult to use and unnecessarily
  complicated, so it was redesigned once before.
- However, the revised version still wasn’t intuitive or user-friendly enough.
- As a result, we received reports of wines being accidentally moved to
  incorrect storage locations.
- In this update, the entire bulk transfer process has been redesigned from the
  ground up.
- The new process consists of three clear steps, making it easier to move wines
  to the intended storage location.
- First, select the destination. Next, choose the wines you want to move.
  Finally, review the source and destination locations along with the selected
  wines before confirming the transfer.
- We sincerely apologize to users who experienced difficulties with this
  feature. We hope you enjoy the redesigned experience, and we would greatly
  appreciate your feedback.

# What’s Next?

- We are currently working on the development of the My Wine Cellar web version
  and its integration with the app.
- We apologize for the delay in releasing this update. We’d also like to give
  you a brief preview of some of the major features planned for upcoming
  versions.
- The next major development phase will begin with version 4.x, with new
  features introduced gradually over time.

## Upcoming Features

### User Accounts

- User account functionality is planned, with email-based login as the primary
  authentication method.
  - Social login options will not be supported.
- User Groups will also be introduced in a future update.
  - This feature is planned for a release after version 4.x.
  - Users will be able to create groups and share wine collections and tasting
    notes with other members.

### Major UI Redesign

- The overall user interface will undergo a significant redesign.
- We are currently planning a completely refreshed experience, starting with the
  main screen and extending throughout the app.

### Wines

- Starting with the web-integrated version, users will be able to retrieve wine
  information directly from the web.
- The initial wine database may have limited coverage, but we will continue
  expanding and improving the available data over time.
- New features related to grape varieties will be introduced.
- The wine summary feature will also be improved to make key information easier
  to understand at a glance.

### Tasting Notes

- With web integration, tasting notes will gain a sharing feature.
- Search filters will become more detailed and customizable.
- New features will analyze users’ preferences based on their tasting notes.

### Wine Cellar

- With web integration, users will also be able to view their cellar information
  on the web.
- The visual presentation will be improved to make cellar management and
  browsing more intuitive.

### Other

- Multilingual support (Japanese and French).
- New wine knowledge and educational content.
- Educational content covering grape varieties and wine aromas.

<br />

We will continue working to bring you more features and improvements.

Thank you for using MaWaCell. We hope you continue to enjoy your wine journey!
